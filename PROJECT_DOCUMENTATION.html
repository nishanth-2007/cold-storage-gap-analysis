1. Executive Summary & Problem Statement

Andhra Pradesh is among India's leading producers of high-value horticultural commodities, including chilli (Guntur/Palnadu), tomatoes (Annamayya/Madanapalle), mangoes (Chittoor/Krishna), bananas (Kadapa), and spices. However, due to severe geographic imbalance in cold storage infrastructure, farmers suffer 18% to 32% post-harvest losses, forcing widespread distress sales during peak harvest seasons.

Existing cold storage databases are often static, fragmented, or inaccessible to ground-level farmers. This project delivers an end-to-end fullstack GIS spatial decision-support platform that connects farmers to available cold storages in real-time, empowers warehouse operators with direct booking management, provides regional planners with mathematical cold chain gap calculations, and enables administrators to govern the state's storage network with strict security.

4. Mathematical Models & Scientific Framework

Formula A: Horticultural Storage Demand Model

Calibrated on NCCD cold chain census norms: 

Demand (MT) = Production (MT) × MS% × RF% × Df
  • MS% (Marketable Surplus): 80% to 92% of yield after farm retention.
  • RF% (Requirement Factor): 30% for bananas up to 60% for fresh chillies.
  • Df (Seasonal Duration Index): Peak harvest concentration coefficient.

Formula B: Road Network Haversine Curvature Model

Converts geodesic coordinates into actual vehicular transit distances: 

Distance (km) = 2R × arcsin(√(a)) × 1.25
  • R (Earth Radius): 6,371 km.
  • 1.25x Factor: Curvature modifier calibrated for AP Highway corridors (NH-16, NH-44, NH-71).

Deficit Severity Index Classification

Severity Tier Deficit Percentage Actionable Impact in Andhra Pradesh
Critical Deficit > 95% Gap Severe farm-gate distress selling. Example: Annamayya tomato belt (98.7% deficit) and Western Kurnool horticulture belt (96.8% deficit).
High Deficit 80% – 95% Gap High post-harvest deterioration. Example: Kadapa Banana Corridor & Chittoor sub-tropical fruit clusters.
Moderate Deficit 50% – 80% Gap Seasonal storage overflows. Northern coastal districts (Anakapalli, Vizianagaram).
Adequate Capacity < 50% Gap Commercialized private cold chain ecosystems. Example: Guntur-Vijayawada spice and cold storage cluster.

5. How Data is Displayed According to User Requirements

To prevent information overload and serve distinct stakeholder requirements, the system dynamically configures its UI into four dedicated personas:

👨‍🌾 Persona 1: Farmer (Discoverability & Direct Action)

  • Requirement: Needs to quickly locate nearby cold storage facilities, check vacancy for their specific crop (chilli, tomato, turmeric), see rental pricing, and book storage space.
  • How Displayed:
    • Proximity Finder: Sorts 200+ facilities by driving distance from farmer's current location or selected district/mandal.
    • Crop Filter: Instant tag filtering displaying only warehouses supporting specific temperature/humidity controls.
    • Interactive Details: One-tap phone dialing, WhatsApp share, pricing per bag/month, and an instant booking form.

🏢 Persona 2: Cold Storage Facility Owner (Asset Management & Influx)

  • Requirement: Needs to register existing or newly constructed warehouses, maintain live chamber vacancy, and manage incoming farmer booking requests.
  • How Displayed:
    • Facility Registration Form: Captures exact GPS lat/lng, licensed capacity (MT), chamber count, approved commodities, and rental rates.
    • Approval Pipeline: When submitted, it goes into a pending_approval status until the administrator reviews it.
    • Farmer Booking Queue: Real-time dashboard to accept, confirm, or decline storage reservation requests.

📊 Persona 3: Regional Infrastructure Planner (Policy & Spatial Allocation)

  • Requirement: Needs to identify where the state government should incentivize new cold chain investments and subsidies.
  • How Displayed:
    • Interactive Gap Analysis Heatmap: District-by-district production vs licensed capacity deficit visualizer.
    • AI Recommended Potential Locations: Algorithmic spatial clusters pinned directly on the GIS map, highlighting high-yield clusters located >25 km from existing cold storages with highway accessibility.

🛡️ Persona 4: System Administrator (Security, Governance & Audit)

  • Requirement: Needs absolute administrative control to approve new facilities, delete fraudulent entries, manage user accounts, and prevent tampering.
  • How Displayed:
    • Protected Direct URL: Hidden dedicated login gate at /admin-login shielded by JWT authentication.
    • Approval Center: Review pending owner submissions with one-click "Approve" (instantly adds the facility to the live public GIS map) or "Reject".
    • Selective Deletion Rights: Admin can delete user-submitted or owner-added facilities, while official government API records remain immutable and protected.

6. Security Architecture, Routing & Verification

  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Standard users and owners cannot access administrative endpoints or consoles. Protected routes redirect unauthorized visits with HTTP 403 Forbidden screens.
  • Secret Administrator Portal: The Admin interface is intentionally separated from common UI navigation and accessible only via direct URL (/admin-login).
  • Single Page Application (SPA) Deep Routing: Express 5 server includes regex middleware routing all non-API GET requests to client/dist/index.html, preventing 404 errors on browser page reloads in cloud deployment.
  • Dynamic Document Title & Custom Favicon: A dedicated PageTitleTracker updates the browser tab dynamically across all pages, complemented by an SVG cold storage warehouse vector favicon.

7. Project Impact & Conclusion

The Andhra Pradesh Cold Storage Infrastructure Mapping & Spatial Gap Analysis Platform successfully bridges the critical information gap between farmers, warehouse operators, and policymakers. By combining authoritative state datasets with dynamic GIS mapping, transparent booking workflows, and algorithmic infrastructure planning, the platform establishes a reproducible blueprint for digital agricultural logistics in India.
